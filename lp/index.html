Your first latte, in three steps

  1. Add matcha and hot water. 2 teaspoons (about 4g) of matcha and about 1.5 oz (40 ml) of water, around 158°F (70°C).
  2. Whisk. Briskly, in an “M” motion, until smooth and foamy.
  3. Pour over milk. 7–8 oz (200–240 ml), with ice if you like. Oat milk is our favorite.

Questions

Do I need a whisk?

A whisk gives the creamiest foam. A handheld milk frother works in a pinch.

How many cups is one tin?

About 7 lattes, using 2 teaspoons (about 4g) each. If you drink it straight, about 15 cups.

How should I store it?

Keep the tin tightly closed in a cool, dark place. If you keep it in the fridge, let it warm to room temperature before opening so moisture doesn’t get in.

Is AYA good for lattes?

Yes. Its flavor comes through the milk. If you mostly drink it straight, try ZEN.
